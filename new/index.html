Hi! I'm Sam. I collage culture through design and art direction.

I’m a Mexican-Jamaican
designer, born in New York
and based in Long Beach, CA.

My practice spans
graphic design, motion,
3D, and art direction.

Art direction is my research into form
and function. I draw on contemporary art,
mid-2000s technology, handcrafts like
collage, and skateboarding culture
to build a visual vocabulary.
