Hi! I'm Sam. I collage culture through design and art direction.

Sam Morgan (he/they) is a
Mexican-Jamaican designer born in New York and based in Long Beach, CA.

Sam’s design practice involves graphic design, motion design, 3d design, and art direction.

Art direction is my research into form and function. Drawing on art history, the structure of classical works, and the found language of skateboarding culture to build a visual vocabulary.
